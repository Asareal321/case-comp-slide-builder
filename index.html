Q&A

Straight answers to what people ask before they install

What's in the Zip?
One SKILL.md file of written instructions for Claude. No code, and nothing runs on your computer.
Can It Use Our Team's Template?
Yes. It reads the fonts and colors your deck already uses, and asks before changing the font or layout.
What if There's No Client Logo?
It uses the company's published brand colors, or picks a color that fits the prompt and tells you so.
Can It Fix a Deck We Started?
Yes. It reads each slide before it edits anything, and keeps your wording unless you ask it to change.
Will Judges Spot It as AI?
It strips the usual tells, like arrow symbols, all-caps labels and stray fonts, before you see the deck.
Can I Use It on My Phone?
Install it on a computer. Once it's on, you can use it anywhere your Claude account works.
