How it works

From case prompt to full deck in four moves, with you making every call

  1. 1Brief

    Upload the case prompt or describe the slide you need. It sets fonts and colors from the client's brand.

    Your callConfirm the client, the ask and the brand.

  2. 2Questions

    One or two at a time, then research to fill the gaps. You never get a questionnaire.

    Your callAnswer, add data, or tell it to skip.

  3. 3Build

    A judge-ready slide, then a choice of fonts and 6 color palettes, shown on a mock before anything changes.

    Your callApprove the slide and pick the look.

  4. 4Polish

    The rest of the deck through a conclusion slide, then a polish pass on every slide.

    Your callKeep or delete the working slides.

The output is real, editable PowerPoint slides you can keep working on in PowerPoint or Google Slides.

Working slides are moved to the back of the deck. Only you decide whether to delete them. 04

Install

Up and running in two minutes. Choose where you use Claude.

  1. Download the zip

    Don't unzip it. Claude wants the .zip file itself.

    eightytwenty.zip
  2. Open Settings

    Click your name, then Settings → Capabilities, and turn on Code execution and file creation.

  3. Upload the skill

    Under Skills, click Upload skill and choose eightytwenty.zip.

  4. Switch it on

    Make sure its toggle is on, then start a new chat.

  1. Download the zip

    Save it anywhere, for example your Downloads folder.

    eightytwenty.zip
  2. Unzip into your skills folder

    Run this in Terminal from that folder:

    mkdir -p ~/.claude/skills && unzip eightytwenty.zip -d ~/.claude/skills/
  3. Restart Claude Code

    It shows up as /eightytwenty.

What's in the zipOne SKILL.md file of written instructions for Claude. No code, and nothing runs on your computer.
Don't have Pro?Skills need a paid Claude plan with code execution. The free plan can't run them yet.
Safari unzipped it?Right-click the eightytwenty folder, choose Compress, and upload that zip.

Appendix: Q&A

The questions judges would ask, answered before they come up

Is it free?
The skill is. Running it needs a Claude plan that supports skills: Pro, Max, Team or Enterprise, or Claude Code.
The upload says the file is invalid.
Upload the .zip as-is. If your browser unzipped it, compress the eightytwenty folder again and upload that.
Claude isn't using the skill.
Check its toggle under Settings → Capabilities → Skills, start a new chat, or ask for "eightytwenty" by name.
Does it make PowerPoint files?
Yes. Real, editable slides you can download and keep working on in PowerPoint or Google Slides.
Can I use it on my phone?
Installing needs Claude.ai on a computer. Once it's on, you can use it anywhere your Claude account works.
Will it make up data?
No. It works from your case prompt and flags anything it can't confirm so you can source it before judges ask.
Skill settings can move between Claude releases. If a menu name differs, look for Skills under Settings. 07
