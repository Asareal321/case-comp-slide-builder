Cookie Policy

This site sets no cookies, so there's nothing to accept

Last updated November 5, 2026. Questions: eightytwenty.tech@gmail.com

We Don't Use Cookies

This site doesn't set cookies, and it doesn't store anything else in your browser either, such as local storage. That's why there's no cookie banner to click through.

How That Works

  • Analytics: PostHog runs in memory only, so it forgets you when you leave the page. It doesn't load at all if your browser sends Global Privacy Control or Do Not Track.
  • Email signup: your address is sent straight to Kit, without cookies.
  • Fonts and images: served from this site, not from a third party.

The Privacy Policy covers what analytics do collect.

Other Sites

Links to Claude, emails from Kit, and other sites you visit from here have their own cookie policies.

If This Changes

If we ever add cookies that aren't strictly necessary, we'll ask for your consent first and update this page.
