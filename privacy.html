Privacy Policy

What eightytwenty collects, why, and how to have it deleted

Last updated November 5, 2026. Questions: eightytwenty.tech@gmail.com

Who We Are

eightytwenty is a free Claude skill and this website, run as an independent project based in Ontario, Canada. It isn't affiliated with or endorsed by Anthropic, Ivey Business School or Western University.

For any privacy question or request, email eightytwenty.tech@gmail.com. That inbox is read by the person responsible for privacy at eightytwenty.

What We Collect and Why

We only collect what we need to run the site, send the emails you ask for, and improve the skill.

WhatWhat it includesWhyStored with
Site analyticsPages viewed, buttons clicked (for example Download or Copy), the site that sent you, browser and device type, and approximate location derived from your IP address.To count visits and downloads and see what works on the page.PostHog (US)
Email listYour email address, when you joined and which form you used.To send the install link and a short note when a new skill ships. Only with your consent.Kit (US)
FeedbackYour rating, comment, any screenshots you attach, and your email only if you add it.To fix and improve the skill, and to reply if you left an email.PostHog and Vercel (US)
Hosting logsIP address, browser and the page requested, kept briefly by our host.To deliver the site and keep it secure.Vercel (US)

Analytics don't use cookies or any other storage on your device, don't recognise you across visits, and don't record your screen. If your browser sends Global Privacy Control or Do Not Track, analytics don't load at all.

What We Don't Do

  • No accounts, passwords or payments.
  • No advertising trackers, and no selling or renting of your information.
  • We never see what you do with the skill in Claude.

Using the Skill in Claude

The download is a single file of written instructions. When you use it, your case prompts, files and slides go to Anthropic under your own Claude account and Anthropic's privacy policy, not to eightytwenty.

Where Your Information Is Stored

PostHog, Kit and Vercel are service providers that process information for us, under their own security and privacy commitments. They store it in the United States, so it may be accessible to authorities there under US law.

How Long We Keep It

  • Email list: until you unsubscribe or ask us to delete it.
  • Feedback and screenshots: only as long as we need them to act on the feedback. Ask anytime and we'll delete them.
  • Analytics: no longer than our PostHog plan's retention period, used only for site statistics.
  • Hosting logs: as set by Vercel, typically a short period.

Your Choices and Rights

  • Unsubscribe from any email with the link at the bottom.
  • Ask to see, correct or delete the information we hold about you, or withdraw your consent, by emailing eightytwenty.tech@gmail.com. We reply within 30 days.
  • Turn off analytics with Global Privacy Control, Do Not Track or a content blocker.

If you're not satisfied with our answer, you can complain to the Office of the Privacy Commissioner of Canada. In Quebec, you can contact the Commission d'accès à l'information. In the EU or UK, you can contact your local data protection authority.

Children

The site is meant for university students and recruits. It isn't directed at children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect their information.

Security

Information travels over encrypted connections, screenshots are kept in private storage, and only eightytwenty can open them. No system is perfectly secure, so please leave confidential case details out of feedback.

Changes

If this policy changes, we'll update the date at the top. If a change affects how we use information you already gave us, we'll ask for your consent first.
